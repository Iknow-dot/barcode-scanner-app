Catalog, three visual directions

The current drawer renders categories as bare text rows and products as an admin-style list with 36 px thumbnails — functional, but it reads like a settings screen, not a product catalog. All three options below keep the exact same behavior (smart search + scan button on top, category drill-down below, tap a product → live 1C price/stock fetch → add-to-cart sheet) and restyle only the presentation layer.

Data reality: many products have no photo — every option must look good imageless.

No stock in the list: live stock arrives only after tapping a product, so rows show price only.

How to read this: all three keep today's search-first flow untouched — they differ only in how categories and product rows look. My pick is Option A: it upgrades every screen state (with or without photos), changes nothing functionally, and is the smallest diff. Option C's grid/list toggle can be layered on later without redoing A.
