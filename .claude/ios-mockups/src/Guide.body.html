iFlow · consultant app on iPhone
Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, applied
iOS-native structure with iknow.ge's palette (green accent, charcoal text, neutral grays), and the iFlow logo and Noto Sans Georgian kept. Quoted rules and numbers come from developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines; "today" figures are measured from the app's current stylesheet. Screen copy uses the app's real Georgian strings; names, stock and prices are sample data.
Tabs are for places, not actions
"Use tabs for navigation, not actions." Today's dock mixes two sections with three actions (scan, search, cart). Now the tab bar holds პროდუქტები and შეკვეთები plus the trailing search tab iOS allows, which opens the catalog. Scanning is the one prominent button on Home. The active order rides above the tab bar instead of hiding in it.

Sheets for tasks, full screen for the camera
Sheets suit a scoped task; for camera views, "consider a full-screen modal." One sheet at a time, with a grabber. The order is a two-step sheet: Cart (1 / 2) moves on with შემდეგი, Delivery (2 / 2) keeps Back leading, and only the last step carries the confirming action.

17 pt text, never below 11 pt
iOS default text is 17 pt, minimum 11 pt. The app sets much of its mobile text at 13–14 px, with labels at 11 and 10 px. These screens use the Dynamic Type defaults below, and product names wrap to a second line instead of clipping to one.
All screens
44 × 44 pt touch targets
Default control size is 44×44 pt, minimum 28×28. Today's add-to-order button is 36 px. Here every stepper half, row, toolbar button and gift toggle is 44 pt tall or more, with roughly 12 pt padding around bezeled controls.
Product · Cart
One accent, never color alone
A color that marks interactivity shouldn't style static text, so prices move from blue to label color. Stock levels carry a word and a glyph ("მცირე ნაშთი" with a warning mark), never just a green or orange number. Contrast: iknow's green #42AE75 is only 2.8:1 with white, under the 3:1 needed even for bold text, so fills use a deeper shade of it, #3A9866 at 3.6:1, with bold labels; small green text uses #296E49, 5.5:1 on the gray background. A selected segment fills with the accent, so the choice reads at a glance.

Labels above fields, one prominent button
Placeholders disappear while typing, so every field keeps a label. Side-by-side fields stack when text grows (the phone-field bug). Client lookup uses tabs for ID, phone and name: the selected tab labels the field and picks the keyboard, the number pad for ID and phone. One or two prominent buttons per view, verb-first. Destructive actions are system red and never primary.
Client lookup · Client create
Glass for floating controls, never content
Apple's Liquid Glass is a layer for the controls and navigation that float above content, and Apple advises against using it in the content layer. So the tab bar, active-order bar, toolbar buttons, segment tracks, sheet action bars and the scanner's camera controls are glass, and content scrolls beneath them. Lists, cards and fields stay solid so text keeps its contrast. A selected segment stays solid green on its glass track, and corner radii are concentric with the controls they hold.
