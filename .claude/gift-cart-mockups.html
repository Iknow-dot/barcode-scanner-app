B1, sized for thumbs

Final direction: the inline gift counter (B1), scaled for one-handed phone use. Every tap target is at least 44×44px (Apple HIG minimum; Material asks 48dp): quantity stepper, gift pill, and gift mini-stepper. The gift controls sit on their own line, with the pill stretching full-width — a big, separated target that can't be confused with the quantity stepper above it. Round-2 compact sizing is kept on the left for comparison.

Both frames are interactive — toggle pills and use the steppers.

What changed vs round 2

Steppers went from 28px to 44px buttons with a wider count readout; the gift pill grew to 44px tall and stretches across the row; the gift mini-stepper matches at 44px with a larger "1 / 3" readout; product name, prices and line totals stepped up ~2px each; row padding and card spacing widened so adjacent targets don't crowd. In Ant Design terms the implementation is size="large" controls (40px) bumped to 44px via the existing cart CSS in index.css — no new components.

Amounts on part-gift rows stay whatever the backend returns today — magenta is only a marker; no pricing behavior is assumed.
